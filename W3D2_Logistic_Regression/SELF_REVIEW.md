## Viva Preparation

### 1. What does R² = 0.85 mean?

The model explains about 85% of the variation in the target variable.

### 2. When does Ridge outperform Lasso and vice versa?

Ridge is useful when most features contribute to prediction and we want to keep them all. Lasso is useful when feature selection is desired because it can reduce some coefficients to zero.

### 3. Assumptions of Linear Regression

- Linearity
- Independent observations
- Constant error variance
- No severe multicollinearity
- Approximately normal residuals for statistical inference
